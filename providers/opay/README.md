## Overview

OPay is one of Nigeria's largest fintech companies, offering digital payment infrastructure, merchant acquiring, wallets, transfers, POS services, and business payment solutions.

Its developer platform enables merchants and enterprise partners to integrate secure online payment acceptance into websites and applications.

## Products

### Checkout API

Accept online payments from customers.

Features include:

- Card payments
- Bank transfers
- Wallet payments
- Payment verification

---

### Merchant Payments

Accept customer payments through OPay merchant channels.

---

### QR Payments

Generate and accept QR code payments.

---

### POS Services

Integrate OPay POS terminals into merchant workflows.
