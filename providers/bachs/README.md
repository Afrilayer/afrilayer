# Bachs API

Payments and billing platform for internet businesses, settling to African bank accounts.

## Overview

Bachs is a full payments and billing platform combining global checkout, subscription billing, usage-based billing, tax handling, and settlement to African bank accounts (NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR) in one integration. Supports cards, mobile money, bank transfer, and stablecoin (USDT/USDC) checkout across Africa, Europe, and North America.

**Note:** Headquarters could not be confirmed from public sources at time of writing — flagged for follow-up rather than guessed.

## Products

- Global Checkout — accept cards, mobile money, bank transfer, and stablecoin payments
- Subscription Billing — recurring billing for SaaS and subscription businesses
- Usage-Based Billing — metered billing for consumption-based pricing
- Tax Handling — automated tax/VAT as Merchant of Record
- Settlement — payout to African bank accounts in local currency
- Dunning — automated failed-payment recovery
