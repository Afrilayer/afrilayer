# Xend Finance API

## Overview

Xend Finance is a decentralized finance (DeFi) platform, backed by Binance
Labs and Google for Startups, that lets credit unions and cooperatives
channel member savings into stablecoin-denominated yield products.
Registered as Xend Tech Limited, based in Enugu, Nigeria. The company has
since expanded beyond its original credit-union product into Asset Chain,
an EVM-compatible Layer-1 blockchain for real-world asset tokenization.

## Products

- **DeFi Yield Farming**: Earn yield on stablecoin savings
- **Credit Union Integration**: Connect credit unions to DeFi
- **Cooperative Savings**: Savings products for cooperatives
- **Cryptocurrency Wallets**: Digital asset wallet services
- **Web SDK**: `@xend-finance/web-sdk` on npm
