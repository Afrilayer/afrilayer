## Overview

Yoco is one of South Africa's leading fintech companies, providing businesses with modern payment infrastructure.

Its platform enables merchants to accept online payments, in-person card payments, payment links, QR payments, and point-of-sale transactions through a unified developer-friendly API.

## Products

### Payments API

Accept online card payments securely.

Features include:

- Payment creation
- Payment confirmation
- Refunds
- Payment status
- Customer payments

---

### Checkout

Hosted checkout pages for businesses that don't want to build payment forms.

---

### Payment Links

Create shareable payment links for customers.

---

### Card Terminals

Integrate physical card machines into business workflows.

---

### POS

Point-of-sale software and hardware integrations.

---

### QR Payments

Accept QR-based payments from supported wallets.

## Authentication

Yoco APIs use **Bearer Token Authentication**.

Example:

```http
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY
```

API keys are generated from the Yoco Dashboard.

---
